**Version 1, 26 November 2026.** If this text ever changes, it gets a new
version at a new address, and this one stays here unchanged.

Thank you for contributing. Before your first pull request to one of
Satchel's repositories can be merged, a bot asks you to agree to this, by
posting a comment. You only do this once in each repository.

## Why there is one

Satchel uses more than one licence on purpose, as [the licensing
section](/what-is-satchel/#licensing) explains. Keeping every contribution
under one copyright holder's control means the code can be offered under
another licence later as well: for example, the agent under the AGPL for
everyone, and under a paid licence for an organisation that can't accept the
AGPL. Without this agreement, changing a licence would need every
contributor to agree, which in practice means it can't be done.

You keep the copyright in what you contribute. You're giving us
permission, not ownership.

company yet. "You" means the person agreeing to it. A "contribution" is
anything you submit to one of those repositories, such as code,

## The editor fork

For [viboko/scratch-editor](https://github.com/viboko/scratch-editor){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, this agreement covers what you contribute to that fork.
Anything you contribute to Scratch's own repositories comes under the
Scratch Foundation's terms, not this. We may offer a change made in the
fork to the Scratch Foundation, and the licence in section 1 lets us do so
under their terms.

## If you're under 18

A parent or guardian needs to read this with you, and agree to it for you
as well, before you sign. Say so in the comment that signs it.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The bot replies with a link to this page. Post a
comment saying exactly:

> I have read the CLA and I agree to it

The bot records your GitHub username, the comment, and when you posted it,
in the repository. If a commit in your pull request isn't linked to your
GitHub account, add its email address to your account first, so the bot can
tell that it's yours.
