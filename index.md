**Satchel** (working title) is a prototype that brings
[Solid](https://solidproject.org/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} storage to
[Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} projects: a child's
projects are saved to their own personal Pod, instead of living only inside
whichever platform hosted that day's coding session. On top of that sits a
sharing dashboard where the child and their guardian decide *together* who
else can see a project — one project at a time, with either of them able to
take it back whenever they like — rather than the all-or-nothing "shared or
not" switch on the Scratch website. A small, replaceable agent carries out
their decisions,
without ever holding the children's work or anyone's password.
